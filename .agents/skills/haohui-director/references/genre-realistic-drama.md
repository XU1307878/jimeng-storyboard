# Realistic and Dramatic Genre Treatments

Use only the selected card. Treat every visual recipe as an option that must be motivated by the script and physical setting.

## Romantic comedy

- Engine: attraction under denial, misunderstanding, social embarrassment or an interrupted intimate action.
- Rhythm: setup → proximity change → involuntary reaction → interruption or incomplete contact.
- Camera: over-shoulder or two-shot to establish distance; a restrained push or focus shift when the relationship changes; reaction often matters more than the initiating action.
- Performance: outward refusal can contrast with feet, shoulders, gaze return or hand hesitation. Keep the contradiction legible without caricature unless the work is explicitly comic.
- Light: soft or backlit romance is optional and must come from windows, practicals or exterior sources. Do not lock every scene to high saturation, four rim lights or permanent glow.

## Prestige romance

- Engine: ordinary interaction accumulating into one ritualized high point.
- Rhythm: restraint and observation first; the visual high point arrives after emotional preparation.
- Camera: long-lens observation, lateral follow, rack focus between two people or an object, then a held reaction rather than constant orbiting.
- Palette: low-saturation neutrals and soft highlight bloom are optional attributes, not a mandatory imitation of a named series.
- Sound: a breath, clothing contact, weather or room tone may carry the turn. Music is never assumed.

## Workplace and industry

- Engine: procedure, competence, project risk, institutional hierarchy and professional choice.
- Stage the work process accurately enough that the audience can understand what changed.
- Stable medium coverage and orderly geometry suit control; a loss of stability can mark a genuine project crisis.
- Use documents, tools, screens or products as consequential props, but keep generated text abstract unless supplied for post-production.
- Separate professional conflict from romance; the job objective remains primary when the script says so.

## Life realism

- Engine: pressure revealed through ordinary behavior, duration, domestic objects and incomplete communication.
- Prefer natural or practical light, sustained spatial observation and environmental sound.
- Let silence, negative space or a character leaving frame carry emotion when that is stronger than a close-up.
- Do not beautify poverty, grief or labor into a glossy commercial look. Do not force handheld motion; a locked frame may be more truthful.

## Rebirth and time shift

- Engine: present action gains new meaning from past knowledge.
- Distinguish timelines with one or two stable signals: palette, sound texture, lens behavior, costume state, aspect treatment or transition motif.
- Memory fragments should answer or complicate the current trigger; they are not decorative montage.
- For a rebirth awakening, show physical orientation and recognition before declaring revenge. Make the viewer understand where and when the character is.

## Comeback and revenge

- Engine: pressure → withheld capability or proof → controlled reveal → consequence.
- Build the reversal from evidence, status, action or a decision already supported by the script.
- Use reaction shots to confirm the new power relationship, but select only reactions that change audience understanding.
- Stability, focal length and vertical placement can encode control; destabilization or spatial displacement can encode collapse. Do not mechanically use low angle for every victor.
- The source's module 10 is mislabeled and unusable; these rules derive from the valid opening and heroine-revenge material.

## Melodrama

- Engine: information gap, incompatible objectives, delayed truth, separation or reunion.
- Let foreground obstruction, distance, missed eyelines or unequal focus express exclusion.
- Emotional intensity does not require constant tears. Use breath, jaw, hand pressure, aborted touch and voice control.
- Warm/cool contrast is optional; it should map to actual spaces, memories or sources rather than label good and bad mechanically.

## Family power

- Engine: inheritance, hidden identity, authority, loyalty and a family order under pressure.
- Space communicates power: distance from the table center, seat height, doorway control, symmetrical placement and access to consequential objects.
- Begin with controlled geometry when the family system is intact. Let composition fragment only when the order genuinely breaks.
- Use key documents or heirlooms as proof objects with clear custody and reaction chain.

## Science fantasy

- Engine: discovery of a rule, cost of an ability, unfamiliar environment or changed causality.
- Establish the normal baseline and spatial scale before the effect changes it.
- Every ability needs a visible trigger, rule-consistent manifestation, environmental reaction, bodily cost or consequence and a stable after-state.
- Do not rely on floating UI or neon as generic science fiction. Use them only when the world contains such technology.

## Suspense and reversal

- Engine: controlled audience knowledge, fair clues, uncertainty and re-interpretation.
- Hide information through viewpoint, obstruction, focus, sound or timing rather than withholding basic geography until it becomes confusing.
- A clue shot must show what can later be recognized without making the solution obvious.
- Surveillance or pursuit may use long-lens compression, foreground cover or restrained handheld motion; use a sudden camera change only at a genuine discovery.
- A reversal must update the meaning of prior evidence. Last-second information injection is useful only when it is legible and earned.

## Heroine revenge

- Engine: pressure → threshold → decisive proof/action → social consequence → next target.
- Use silence immediately before the decisive act when the surrounding sound bed makes the contrast meaningful.
- Proof close-ups replace explanatory speeches when the object can carry the information.
- Group reaction should follow an intelligible hierarchy: principal opponent, power broker, then restrained crowd response.
- Separate past and present consistently. Do not turn every victorious beat into identical slow-motion entrance imagery.
