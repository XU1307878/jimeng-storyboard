# Animation and Spectacle Genre Treatments

Use these modes only after the user or project establishes the medium. A genre label never authorizes 3D, CG, photorealism or a specific render engine by itself.

## Xianxia combat

- Organize action by an energy unit: ignition → accumulation → release/contact → environmental aftershock → held end state.
- Give every signature technique a stable source, color/material behavior, path, scale, interaction rule and disappearance or residue rule.
- Scale comes from foreground/midground/background relation and environmental reaction, not from adjective repetition.
- Contrast upward and downward force, stillness and eruption, or small practitioner and large phenomenon when the script supports it.
- Keep weapon anatomy and hand contact stable. Split a clip before combining several incompatible techniques.

## Poetic ink CG

- Engine: moral choice, memory, growth or conflict expressed through restraint and visual cadence.
- Treat action rhythm like brushwork: preparation, moving stroke, decisive pause and quiet finishing stroke.
- Use negative space, atmospheric depth, silhouette, controlled particles or ink-like edge behavior only if the selected medium supports them.
- Dialogue and emotion stay restrained. A grand event should often resolve back to one human gaze, breath, hand or footprint.
- Do not force combat spectacle into reflective passages or copy the identity of a named title.

## Grounded wuxia

- Engine: survival, loyalty, historical disorder, physical skill and consequence.
- Combat is fast, close, dirty and costly; confrontation before violence can be very slow.
- Show worn fabric, damp earth, metal abrasion, fatigue, breath, blood or injury only to the degree supported by the script and content requirements.
- Use wide close action for spatial impact, then a held consequence. Avoid weightless flying or excessive magical trails unless the story explicitly introduces them.
- Let markets, inns, weather, animals and labor supply a human breathing interval between fights.

## 3D comedy

- Structure a comic unit as setup → escalation → pre-impact gap → failure/reveal → reaction → freeze or tag.
- The opening may show the abnormal result or peak of overconfidence if it comes from the actual scene.
- Use one large gag with smaller supporting beats. Reaction and sound timing often carry more comedy than extra camera motion.
- Elastic deformation, speed changes and exaggerated facial poses are allowed only in an explicitly stylized 3D comedy medium.
- Keep anticipation, contact and recovery readable; do not let squash-and-stretch destroy identity or prop continuity.
- Module 22 is the current source; module 18 is treated as an earlier compatible draft.

## Mythic dark epic

- Engine: sacrifice, oppression, monstrous scale, destiny and material suffering.
- Use monumental geometry, chiaroscuro, weathered surfaces and small-human/large-world contrast when the story needs tragic grandeur.
- Forceful action follows `weight → three beats → trace`: preparation, contact, aftershock, plus a visible mark on body, prop or environment.
- Silence or slow motion belongs to a decisive irreversible instant, not every strike.
- Translate any named-filmmaker reference into observable attributes. Do not output imitation requests.

## Medieval underworld fantasy

- Activate only when the story genuinely combines streetwise team behavior, medieval power geography and rough fantasy action.
- Plan or recollection may alternate a speaker with very short imagined-action inserts, but the audience must know which images are hypothetical.
- Use dirty rain, oil, leather, rough cloth, fire practicals and vertical city geography to contrast street and tower power.
- Speed-ramped impact can contrast normal pursuit, a brief decisive slow instant and immediate return to normal speed. Do not hard-code frame rates unless the platform or user provides them.
- Modern streetlike body rhythm inside period costume is an optional characterization choice, never a default for all medieval stories.

## Black-gold CG

- Engine: suppression → trigger → awakening → confrontation or terminal release.
- Keep black, gold and any third accent color narratively assigned. Gold should not bloom at full strength before the story earns it.
- State each effect element as a physical system: origin, material, motion, interaction, light contribution and residual decay.
- Use wide composition to express encirclement or power scale and close detail to show the bodily price.
- Cloth, hair, weapon and effect motion need anticipation, lag, overshoot and settling so the CG action does not stop mechanically.
- Source instructions that ban music or fix an aspect ratio are not universal. User and platform choices govern.
